# Club-first copy implementation gaps

Status date: 18 August 2026

This record separates approved copy from mechanisms that the current product
and operating material do not yet support. Public pages must not present these
items as live until the closure evidence exists.

## Operational claims not yet verified

- Public application intake is not active. The application page states that
  privacy and consent wording remains under review.
- Invitation and membership state logic exists in code and tests, but no live
  invitation or application workflow is connected to the public site.
- Mutual-interest reveal logic exists in code and tests, but no complete UI,
  persistence and operational workflow is connected to it.
- Journey-specific suitability is free-text only. There is no structured
  physical, practical, medical, experience or documentation decision process.
- Sponsored-guest suitability and conduct rules do not exist.
- The member code of conduct remains a legal-review item.
- Journey assessment and material-change reassessment have no implemented
  workflow. Current assessment dates are null.
- Named contracting, payment, emergency and escalation responsibilities are
  absent from the journey data.
- Local-partner payment is recorded as a requirement to contract, not as a
  completed fact.
- A public Archive explanation now exists, but no authenticated Archive route,
  content store or member contribution workflow exists.
- Travel Self attribution for Archive contributions cannot be verified until
  the Archive exists.

## Missing journey responsibility data

The following fields are missing for the complete Andean Caravan, each of its
four canonical sections and The Stone Road short-form exception:

- Conceived by
- Hosted by
- Operated by
- Contracting party
- Payment received by
- Assessed by Sawayatra
- Sawayatra's role
- Emergency and escalation responsibility
- Last reviewed

The same nine fields are missing for each of the nine Egyptian Western Desert
guides. Those guides name no operator, contracting party, payment recipient or
emergency responsibility and remain draft source material rather than admitted
Sawayatra programme journeys.

## Publication rule

A responsibility panel may be published only when all nine fields carry
approved values and evidence. Incomplete records remain in development data and
must not render as placeholders, defaults or implied Sawayatra responsibility.
