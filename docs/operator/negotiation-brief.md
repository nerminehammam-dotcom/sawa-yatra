# Operator negotiation brief — items the spec cannot settle alone

Nothing here is agreed; these are the points the operator agreement must
cover before the matching tokens can be signed. No terms have been
fabricated in the build.

1. **Replacement deadline (§7.7 / REPLACEMENT_DEADLINE).** The last point a
   seat may transfer to a replacement member, at or before the lock date —
   bounded by what the agreement allows for manifests, permits and rooming
   changes. Also needed: the manifest/rooming change window itself
   (SUBSTITUTION_NOTICE gives the group time to raise a substitution with
   the host).

2. **Cancellation fee retention (§7.7 / CANCELLATION_TERMS,
   SHORTFALL_HOLDER).** Whether cancellation fees are retained by Sawayatra
   so they can be applied first to holding the remaining travellers' price.
   If the operator retains them, the fee-holds-price mechanism needs a
   different funding route, or the group re-bands (REBAND_NOTICE) — and
   whichever is chosen must be stated on the section page before anyone
   books.

3. **Quorum-call candidate dates (§6.5).** Candidate dates offered in a
   quorum call must be dates the operator can actually run — never
   invented. Needed: the process by which Sawayatra obtains operator-
   feasible date sets per section and window, and how firm they are.

4. **Pricing inputs (§11.2).** Operator fixed cost per section, per-head
   cost, duration, inclusions, currency, floor/ceiling — the pricing
   agent's inputs. Its output is indicative only and lands in
   pending-review/; nothing reaches a member surface without sign-off.

5. **Upgrades and single occupancy (§7.8).** Which nights and gate cities
   are upgradeable (and explicitly which are not), per-person per-section
   pricing, and single-occupancy terms — named plainly on the section page.
