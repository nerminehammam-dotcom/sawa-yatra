# Sawayatra — Visual & Voice Constitution

v2.5 · 8 August 2026 Supersedes v2.4, v2.3, v2.2, v2.1 and v2.0 (8 August 2026) and the working reference of 7 August 2026.

## 0. How this document works

Scope. This governs imagery, typography, colour, motion and voice — everywhere Sawayatra publishes: the site, the questionnaire, route material, printed matter, decks and social. It governs new work, and any existing asset the moment it is next touched.

It does not govern layout and grid, icon system, video, or diagrams other than maps. Those live in the identity and layout specifications. Where those specifications and this document disagree on imagery, type, colour, motion or voice, this document wins.

Precedence. Where this document and any other Sawayatra document disagree on a matter in scope, this one wins. Where it and the code disagree, the code is wrong and gets corrected — except in §7 Colour, where styles/tokens.css is the source of truth and this document is a summary of it.

Nothing here is a suggestion. There are no open clauses. Where a decision could reasonably have gone the other way, the reasoning is stated so it can be overturned deliberately rather than eroded quietly. Overturning a clause means editing this file and bumping the version — not making an exception on a single page.

Exceptions. Any departure from any clause in §§1–10 is either refused or logged in Appendix B with a date, the asset or copy, the clause, the reason and the approver. (§11 only summarises; there is nothing there to except.) The one clause that admits no exception is §3.1's prohibition on stock or generated imagery being presented as documentary evidence of a place Sawayatra travels. (That is the whole of the absolute. It does not reach the illustration system, which is governed by §4.) An unlogged exception is a defect. If Appendix B reaches five entries against one rule, the rule is wrong and gets rewritten.

The gate. This document is the tie-breaker, but nobody opens a tie-breaker at the moment they are least likely to think they need one. The six-line pre-flight (sawayatra-asset-preflight.md) lives wherever assets are committed and is the operational form of §§2–10. Passing the pre-flight is the condition of publication.

Amendment. Version, date, and a one-line changelog at the bottom. Principles live in §§1–11 and are expected to hold for years. Tasks live in Appendix A and are expected to rot; a stale Appendix A does not invalidate the body.

## 1. The governing rule

Every image, type choice and motion has a single assigned job.

Sawayatra has several strong visual languages

1. documentary photography,
2. gouache illustration,
3. cut-paper cartography.

Any two of them competing for the same job produces three brands instead of one.
Separation of duties is what makes them read as a system.

If a proposed asset does not fit a job in §2, it does not go in.

## 2. Image classes

Six classes. Every use of an image is assigned one primary class before the image is chosen.

| Class | Job | Material |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Route evidence | This place exists, and we have been there | Sawayatra photography, made on the journey it describes |
| Practical | What you will actually sleep in, ride in, and eat | Photography, clearly practical in framing, never dressed as editorial |
| Portrait | Who is behind this / who hosts | Photography, consented, named |
| Imagination | Interior states, Travel Selves, "anywhere" | Illustration |
| Orientation | Where you are on the route | Cartography |
| None | The sentence is doing the work | White space |

The class belongs to the placement, not to the file. A frame may carry secondary material — a consented host portrait will inevitably also show the route; an accommodation photograph is also evidence of a place. That is not a violation. What is required is that placement, caption and treatment make one job unmistakably dominant in each use. The same frame may be route evidence on the caravan page and practical on a logistics page, provided each use is unambiguous on its own.

What never mixes is material, not subject matter. A Travel Self does not stand in a real named place. A route photograph does not carry an illustrated overlay. A map is not decoration.

"None" is a real class and the default when nothing qualifies. A page short of imagery is not a page short of an image; it is a page whose photography does not exist yet. Filling the gap is how the promise in §3 dies.

## 3. Photography

### 3.1 The promise

The public line is scoped so that it stays literally true as the club grows:

Every route photograph on this site is the work of Nermine Hammam, made on the journey it describes.

That is the claim to defend, and it is the single hardest thing on this site for anyone else to acquire. It is not travel documentation that happens to have been taken by the founder; it is an artist's body of work made along the route, continuous with an existing practice. A competitor can copy a proposition, palette, and tone of voice. They cannot copy this. It covers route evidence. It does not extend to practical or portrait photography, which are separately placed and never presented as editorial route material.

Absolute, no exception, not logged as an exception: no stock photography and no generated imagery is ever presented as documentary evidence of a place Sawayatra travels. This one does not bend for a launch date.

### 3.2 Grade

The photography is authored work, and the treatment is part of the work.

The aged and worked register in these frames is not a filter applied after the fact. It is how the photographs are made, and it is continuous with a practice that has its own long-standing relationship to surface, age and colour. It is not available for correction by anyone but the artist.

An earlier version of this document rejected the aged register on the grounds that paper and age already belong to the interface and the illustration, and that three materials in one costume weaken photography's job as evidence. That reasoning had the direction of influence backwards. The practice precedes the brand. The interface's warmth and the poster series' paint are downstream of the photography's register, not rivals to it. The clause was overruled by the author, which is what §0 provides for.

What the earlier clause was reaching for, and what still holds:

Authored variation, not accidental variation. A body of work has a range. A heavily worked frame and a clean one belong in the same series when both are decisions. What breaks a series is the frame that reads as a preset, a legacy export or a compromise — variation the viewer experiences as inconsistency rather than as range. The test is not uniformity; uniformity would flatten the work. The test is whether someone moving through the site can feel one hand.

The reference triptych is still worth making, with a changed purpose. Not a target every frame is corrected to, but a statement of the range's edges: the most worked frame that still belongs, the cleanest frame that still belongs, and one between them. Frames outside those edges are reworked or dropped. Nothing is graded toward an average.

The evidence job survives the treatment. A worked photograph is still a document — the place was there, the artist was there. What must never happen is treatment heavy enough that a viewer cannot tell whether they are looking at a photograph or a painting, because §2's whole architecture depends on that distinction being legible at a glance. Where a frame approaches that line, it is reworked or it is not published. It is not reclassified as imagination. Imagination is an illustration class, and a heavily worked photograph does not become a gouache by losing legibility. The material separation is what the class system rests on; nothing is permitted to erode it from either side.

### 3.3 Rights and consent

Every published photograph has a recorded maker, date, location and rights status. No image is published whose rights cannot be stated in one line.

Go to the metadata of all images and add the date and location. All photos are done by Nermine Hammam, in this phase of the website build.

Public-scene photography where individuals are incidental and unidentifiable does not require individual consent, but is subject to §3.4.

Contributor and member-submitted photography is licensed in writing before use, credited adjacent to the image or in its caption, never burned into the frame and never graded to pass as Sawayatra route evidence.

### 3.5 Members

No member is ever photographed for publication. Members appear as Travel Selves, illustration, words, symbols.

A member who creates or hosts a journey remains a member for this purpose. Hosting does not convert a member into a portrait subject; they appear as a Travel Self, and the portrait class covers non-member hosts only. This is a promise of the club, not a stylistic preference, and it is why §11 reads "documented route, host or consented subject → photograph" rather than "person → photograph."

### 3.6 Attribution, and the line against portfolio

This section governs attribution of Nermine Hammam's route photography. Contributor practical and portrait photography keeps per-image credit adjacent to the image or in its caption, under §3.3.

The work is credited, and the authorship is stated plainly — it is a reason to trust the club, not a decoration.

But Sawayatra is not a portfolio. Credit appears once, where a reader would go looking for it — a stated line on the photography or about page — and not frame by frame across the site. Per-image artist credit turns route evidence into a gallery wall and moves the viewer's attention from the place to the picture. On this site the photographs are working: they are showing someone where they would be standing. The authorship is what makes that showing trustworthy, not what the page is about.

Exhibition, print and portfolio contexts are outside this document's scope and are governed by the artist's own practice.

### 3.7 Craft

One caption system: same voice, same length discipline. (Alt text is not caption text; see §10.)

Landscapes keep the horizon.

## 4. Illustration

The gouache travel-poster series is for the imagination class only: Travel Selves, conceptual mastheads, "anywhere" ideas.

Never where a real, named place is being described. Real place is photography's job, and if the photograph does not exist, §2 "None" applies.

One hand throughout: one palette, one paint texture, one lettering treatment.

Poster lettering is drawn, not typeset. It is a hand-painted treatment held consistent across the series, and it is not governed by §6 — Fraunces and General Sans are not painted, and painted letters are not a third typeface. Where drawn lettering carries information rather than atmosphere, it is duplicated as real text (§10).

Illustration carries no factual load. Nothing a traveller needs to know may exist only inside a painting.

## 5. Cartography

The animated route map is a signature object and stays scarce. Cut-paper cartographic language is used only for orientation and progress.

Never as decorative pattern, background, section divider or texture. Scarcity is what keeps it signature.

It exports one motion vocabulary to the rest of the site (§8) and nothing else.

Every map has a static, labelled equivalent carrying the same information (§10).

## 6. Typography

### 6.1 The wordmark

The master Sawayatra wordmark is a locked artwork asset. It is never retyped, reconstructed from a live font, stretched, outlined, recoloured outside the palette, or rebuilt page by page. Clear space and minimum sizes are held in the identity specification; this clause exists so that no typographic decision below is ever read as licence to redraw it.

### 6.2 Two faces, two registers

Fraunces — display, headings, pull quotes, editorial body: the voice of the club.

General Sans — operational text: navigation, forms, questionnaire, buttons, tables, prices, dates, itineraries, exclusions, captions, legal.

Reasoning: one voice does not require one typeface. Fraunces carrying nav, data and dense operational text is the point at which a strong display face becomes mannerism, and operational text is precisely where personality costs comprehension for no return. The register split also matches the voice split in §9 — expressive language in the expressive face, literal language in the plain one — which makes the rule easy to apply and easy to see when broken.

### 6.3 Mechanics

This subsection tracks implementation and is expected to change more often than the rest of the body. It is kept here rather than devolved to a spec because the cost of a stale token name is seconds, while the cost of losing an undocumented rendering constraint is an afternoon.

Scale tokens only. --fs-display-1…3, --fs-hero, --fs-heading, --fs-card, --fs-title, --fs-lead, --fs-body, --fs-small. New pages consume tokens; bespoke clamp() values are a defect, not a preference.

A genuinely new role gets a new semantic token and an amendment to this file — never a hard-coded page value. If the ramp is missing something, the ramp is what changes.

Optical size does the register work within Fraunces. It does not substitute for the face split above.

Eyebrows use the shared Eyebrow component (0.12em tracking, ≥0.875rem). Small caps are not re-rolled per page.

Fraunces headings stay at weight ≥500; below that the display cut's lowercase e breaks at 1x.

Arrows are the `<Arrow />` SVG, never a font glyph.

Prices, dates and durations use tabular figures.

## 7. Colour

Eight colours. styles/tokens.css is the source of truth; every pairing's contrast is documented and CI-enforced.

A ninth colour requires an entry in the contrast matrix in the same commit. No matrix entry, no colour.

--signal (orange) is a fill behind ink, or a large mark. Never text, icon or hairline on paper — it fails 3:1.

--dusty-blue / --soft-blue are true blue. (They previously aliased olive; corrected.)

Hierarchy comes from size, weight and optical size. Adding a colour to create emphasis is the failure mode this section exists to prevent.

No information is carried by colour alone (§10).

## 8. Motion

prefers-reduced-motion support is preserved exactly as implemented, and every animated element has a static state that is complete on its own.

The only motion vocabulary is the map's: a line drawing forward, a section filling as it is reached. Motion means "you are moving along the route." Nothing else is a reason to move.

Functional state changes are exempt from the metaphor, not from restraint. A menu opening, an accordion expanding, a field validating: these may be immediate or carry a brief, non-expressive transition. They do not introduce a second motion vocabulary, and they never become expressive.

No parallax, no decorative fades, no scroll-jacking, no autoplaying carousels.

No information is carried by motion alone.

## 9. Voice

Three registers. The register is determined by the text's purpose, not by its position on the page.

### 9.1 Editorial — the club speaking

Understated, two-beat lines. "Go alone, arrive together."

Confidence through restraint. No hype, no luxury-travel cliché, no exclamation marks.

British spelling: travelling, colour, recognise.

Honesty as a feature. When something is not ready, say so plainly — "The details come after the idea is clear" — rather than faking it.

Mechanisms, never guarantees. Describe how the club works ("introduced only when it's mutual"); never promise an outcome.

Never invent a claim. Prices, benefits, FAQs, testimonials and safety facts wait for real, approved content. Placeholders stay honest until then.

Failure modes, named so they can be caught in draft: the sentence that sounds like a brochure; the compressed line that has become obscure rather than restrained; the claim smuggled in as atmosphere ("unforgettable", "life-changing", "the trip of a lifetime"); the guarantee wearing a mechanism's clothes ("you'll find your people").

### 9.2 Operational — prices, dates, exclusions, visas, health, cancellation, safety

Plain, complete, literal. The two-beat cadence is abandoned here, deliberately. Ambiguity in a cancellation condition is not atmosphere; it is a dispute. Long sentences are acceptable if they are unambiguous. Set in General Sans (§6).

One source of truth. Every price, date, duration and condition has exactly one structured origin, and every appearance on the site reads from it. Key facts are restated at each decision point — a traveller should never have to scroll back to learn what they are agreeing to — but restatement is rendering, never retyping. Two hand-written copies of a number is how a site develops two prices.

The editorial voice's restraint is a virtue in editorial and a liability in operations. Anyone editing operational copy for elegance is introducing a defect.

### 9.3 Member — journeys created by members

Members write in their own voice and are not edited into Sawayatra's. Member text is visually distinguished from club text so a reader always knows who is speaking. Sawayatra's editorial standards apply to what is published (accuracy, representation), not to cadence or style.

### 9.4 Punctuation

The em dash is approved in editorial voice, unspaced, used sparingly. It is not used in operational copy, where a full stop or a semicolon is clearer.

## 10. Accessibility floor

Not a compliance appendix. These are the conditions under which the rest of this document is legible at all.

Sawayatra does not design for a demographic, and nothing in this section is an accommodation for a group. The club is open to travellers of any age, and reading conditions vary far more than readers do: a tablet held at arm's length, a phone in a moving bus, bright altitude light, a laptop at the end of a long day. Generous defaults serve everyone in those conditions, which is why they are the baseline rather than an option.

Type and target

- Sustained body text 18px (1.125rem). 16px is permitted only for short, secondary information — never for anything a traveller reads for more than a sentence or two.
- Line height ≥1.5 for body; 1.55–1.65 for long operational copy.
- Measure capped at ~70 characters.
- Interactive targets ≥48×48px with visible spacing between them.

Structure

- Keyboard focus is always visible and never suppressed; focus order follows reading order.
- Text over photography requires a scrim or a clear plate. Contrast is measured against the actual pixels behind the text at every breakpoint, not against an average.
- All contrast pairings meet the matrix in §7; --signal restrictions apply everywhere, including hover and active states.
- No information conveyed by colour alone or motion alone.
- Every map, chart and animated route has a static, labelled text equivalent conveying the same information.
- Captions and map labels meet the same contrast and size rules as body text. Drawn lettering that carries information is duplicated as real text (§4).

Alt text

- Every image receives an explicit alt decision. There is no default and no house opening phrase.
- Informative images — a concise description of what matters in this context. The same photograph may need different alt text in two placements, because its job differs (§2).
- Functional images — icons, image links, buttons — describe the function, not the appearance. "Open the route map", not "A folded paper map".
- Decorative images — empty alt (alt=""), so screen readers pass over them rather than announcing noise.
- Never a filename, never auto-generated, never a duplicate of an adjacent caption.

## 11. The constitution, in one line each

- Documented route → Nermine Hammam's photography, made on the journey described, within the authored range, rights recorded.
- Host or consented non-member subject → portrait photography, consented, named where permission allows, rights recorded. Not covered by the authorship promise.
- Member identity or Travel Self, including a member who hosts → illustration, words or symbol — never a member portrait.
- Idea, imagination, interior state → illustration (one hand, no factual load).
- Where you are on the route → map (orientation only, static equivalent always).
- Nothing that qualifies → no image.
- One primary job per use; the class belongs to the placement, not the file.
- Editorial type → Fraunces, from the scale tokens. The wordmark is locked artwork.
- Operational type → General Sans, tabular figures, one source of truth.
- Colour → the closed eight, contrast-checked; --signal fills only.
- Motion → reduced-motion first; the route line is the only vocabulary.
- Editorial words → understated, British, honest, mechanisms-not-guarantees.
- Operational words → plain, complete, literal; cadence abandoned on purpose.
- Every image → an explicit alt decision, informative, functional or empty.
